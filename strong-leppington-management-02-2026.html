Executive Summary
Current State

Membership: Leppington's membership base recorded a net gain of 3 members across the quarter (322 → 325), reversing the prior period's net loss of 7. The active rate fluctuated between 71.7% in December and 79.8% in January before settling at 76.3% in February, with attrition contained below the sub-7% target across all three months.

Revenue: The studio generated $204,589 in gross revenue across the quarter, with January peaking at $75,738 before February closed at $63,436 — a 16.2% decline. Membership revenue dominated at 85.4%–90.1% monthly. Late cancel fees eased from $3,542 in January to $2,721 in February, a positive signal for improving attendance behaviour.

Utilisation: Session attendance metrics improved consistently across the quarter, with February the first month to simultaneously achieve both booking utilisation (79.93%) and attendance utilisation (71.25%) within their respective target ranges. The booking-to-attendance gap held below 10% across all three months (8.17%–8.85%), indicating stable member follow-through on bookings.

Sales vs Cancellations: Membership sales built from 11 in December to 28 in February — the strongest monthly result in six months — delivering net positive membership movement in both January (+3) and February (+7). January's 109 Starter Pass pipeline appears to have converted in February, with 56 intro offers sold and first visits holding at 76.

Key Issues
  • February revenue softness: Gross revenue declined 16.2% from January's $75,738 to $63,436, the softest month of the quarter requiring monitoring.
  • Active rate pullback: February's active rate retreated to 76.3% as suspended members increased from 65 to 77, after January's strong 79.8% recovery.
  • Attendance utilisation sustainability: February hit the 70–75% target (71.25%) for the first time — sustaining this into March is the key test.
  • Average revenue per visit decline: Revenue per visit fell from $22.78 in January to $18.52 in February, with intro offer mix driving the lower per-visit rate.
  • Attrition creep: February's 6.56% attrition, while below sub-7% target, represents a gradual uptick from December's 4.79% requiring continued retention focus.
Active Interventions Being Deployed
  • FOH Weekly Schedule adherence: FOH team currently aligning fully with scheduled task framework, ensuring consistent daily execution across all sales and member engagement touchpoints.
  • Sales pipeline currency: Active focus on bringing the full sales pipeline completely up to date by end of March, ensuring no leads or follow-ups fall through.
  • Social media execution: All studio socials being executed as per established guidelines, supporting lead generation and community engagement heading into the next quarter.
  • Recovr retention software: Systematic retention and member engagement tracking supporting attrition management and follow-up on at-risk members.
  • Operational alignment programme: Next quarter focus on full studio-level alignment across FOH, socials, event planning, and LAM — all milestones pre-planned ahead of July slow-down period.
Expected Outcomes
  • Sustain utilisation gains: Maintain February's booking (79.93%) and attendance (71.25%) utilisation within target ranges through consistent member engagement and FOH execution.
  • Stabilise revenue: Support recovery from February's $63,436 toward January's $75,738 baseline through membership growth and pipeline conversion activity.
  • Contain attrition: Hold attrition below sub-7% target and work toward sub-6% to ensure membership sales consistently deliver net positive growth.
  • Build operational foundation: Full FOH, socials, event planning, and LAM alignment ahead of July slow-down period, building past prior year's growth trajectory.
  • Pipeline currency: Complete sales pipeline update by month end, ensuring all leads and conversion opportunities are actively managed heading into Q2.

Utilisation Trend
Insight

January served as the transition month, with booking at 74.29% and attendance at 65.44% before February delivered the breakthrough.

The booking-to-attendance gap held below the 10% threshold across all three months (8.17%, 8.85%, 8.68%), indicating stable member follow-through on bookings throughout the period. Average revenue per visit declined from $22.63 in December to $18.52 in February — an 18.1% drop — likely reflecting the increased presence of intro offer participants attending at lower per-visit revenue. Sustaining February's attendance utilisation within the 70–75% healthy range into March is the immediate priority.

Summary & Path Forward
Insight

Leppington closed the December–February quarter on an improving trajectory. Membership returned to net positive growth (322 → 325), February delivered both utilisation targets for the first time, and membership sales reached their strongest monthly result in six months at 28 units. Revenue softened in February to $63,436, though this occurred against a backdrop of strengthening membership fundamentals, easing late cancel fees, and the booking-to-attendance gap holding consistently below 10%.

The next quarter's focus is full operational alignment across FOH, socials, event planning, and LAM — building past the prior year's growth ahead of the July slow-down. The FOH team is working through complete adherence to the Weekly Schedule, the sales pipeline is being brought fully up to date, and social media is executing per established guidelines. Sustaining February's utilisation and membership sales gains will be the defining measure of the quarter ahead.

Key Takeaways
  • Quarter closed on an improving trajectory — net membership gain of 3, February's strongest membership sales in six months, and first simultaneous achievement of both utilisation targets
  • Revenue softened in February ($63,436) but occurred alongside strengthening membership fundamentals and improving attendance behaviour
  • Next quarter focus centres on full operational alignment across FOH, socials, event planning, and LAM to build past prior year's growth ahead of the July slow-down
  • FOH Weekly Schedule adherence, pipeline currency, and social media execution are the active operational priorities as of early March
