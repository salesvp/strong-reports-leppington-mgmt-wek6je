EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Membership

Leppington ended January 2026 with 322 total members, comprising 257 active and 65 suspended members. The active rate improved to 79.8%, up from 71.7% in December following seasonal recovery. The quarter resulted in net negative growth of 7 members, with 50 membership sales across the three months offset by 56 cancellations.

Revenue

Quarterly gross revenue totalled $218,930 across November-January. November delivered $77,777, December declined to $65,415 during the holiday period, and January recovered to $75,738. Membership revenue dominated the revenue mix, contributing between 76.8% and 90.1% monthly. Package sales normalised from Black Friday-driven 97 units in November to baseline 35-37 units in December-January. Intro offer revenue increased from $1,881 in November to $5,921 in January driven by the STRONG Starter Pass promotion.

Utilisation

Booking utilisation averaged 70.80% across the quarter, achieving the optimal 70-85% target range in November (72.53%) and January (74.29%) but dipping to 65.59% in December. Attendance utilisation remained below the 70-75% healthy threshold throughout the period, ranging from 57.42% in December to 65.44% in January. The 8-9 percentage point gap between booking and attendance utilisation reflects ongoing challenges with late cancellations and no-shows.

Sales vs Cancellations

The quarter delivered 50 membership sales (17 in November, 11 in December, 22 in January) against 56 cancellations, resulting in net loss of 6 members. Intro offer sales surged 203% from 36 units in November to 109 in January, driven by the STRONG Starter Pass promotion that built a conversion pipeline expected to materialise in mid-to-late February. Package sales totalled 169 units across the quarter, with November's Black Friday promotion accounting for 97 of those units.

Key Issues

  • Net negative membership growth: 56 cancellations exceeded 50 sales across the quarter, creating net loss of 7 members (329 → 322) despite attrition rates within acceptable range
  • Attrition management: While rates of 4.79-6.14% achieved the sub-7% target, the net negative growth demonstrates need for slight improvement to avoid relying on exaggerated membership sales to offset cancellations
  • Attendance utilisation below threshold: All three months fell short of the 70-75% healthy range (63.93% → 57.42% → 65.44%), with 8-9 point gap between bookings and actual attendance indicating late cancel and no-show challenges
  • Revenue per visit decline: Average dropped from $24.16 in November to $22.78 in January, likely reflecting increased trial activity with starter pass bookings not generating proportionate revenue growth
  • December seasonal impact: Revenue declined 15.9% and active rate dropped to 71.7% during the holiday period, demonstrating vulnerability to seasonal headwinds

Active Interventions Being Deployed

  • Recovr retention software: Supporting systematic retention efforts and pipeline management to reduce cancellations and improve member engagement tracking
  • STRONG Starter Pass promotion: Generated 109 intro offer sales in January, building conversion pipeline expected to deliver membership growth in mid-to-late February
  • STRONG Intro Offer transition: Mid-February launch of 5 days for $50 offer which testing has shown increases conversion rates from trial to membership
  • STRONG Challenge: Launching this month to drive class attendance, member engagement, and retention through gamified participation and community building
  • FOH restructure: New structure creating seamless customer journey with ongoing refinement and training to optimise sales pipeline efficiency and conversion processes
  • Ongoing sales training: Collaboration with FOH to ensure routines are built around daily scheduled tasks and proficiency with software and processes as per sales pipelines

Expected Outcomes

  • Maintain attrition within the 4-6% range while incrementally improving to ensure sales consistently exceed cancellations without requiring exaggerated membership volumes
  • Convert January's 109 intro offer pipeline into membership growth in mid-late February through improved conversion processes and new 5 days for $50 offer structure
  • Improve attendance utilisation from 65% toward 70-75% healthy threshold through better booking behaviour and reduced late cancellations, strengthening revenue per visit and member stickiness
  • Stabilise membership base at net positive growth through combination of sustained low attrition (sub-7% range) and consistent sales execution (20+ memberships monthly)
  • Execute consistent sales and retention processes through FOH restructure and training that support systematic pipeline management and member engagement

SUMMARY & PATH FORWARD

Leppington concluded the November-January quarter with 322 members, down from 329 in November, as 56 cancellations across the period outpaced 50 membership sales. The studio generated $218,930 in quarterly revenue with membership revenue maintaining a dominant 76.8-90.1% share across all three months. December presented expected seasonal challenges with revenue declining to $65,415 and the active member rate dropping to 71.7%, though January demonstrated recovery with revenue rebounding to $75,738 and the active rate improving to 79.8%. The STRONG Starter Pass promotion drove intro offer sales from 36 in November to 109 in January, building a conversion pipeline expected to materialise in mid-to-late February.

The studio's operational metrics showed capacity for growth within current infrastructure. Booking utilisation averaged 70.80% across the quarter, achieving the optimal 70-85% target in November (72.53%) and January (74.29%). However, attendance utilisation remained below the healthy 70-75% threshold throughout the period (63.93% → 57.42% → 65.44%), with an 8-9 percentage point gap between bookings and actual attendance indicating ongoing challenges with late cancellations and no-shows. Attrition rates ranged from 4.79% to 6.14%, remaining above the sub-7% target and requiring systematic retention interventions to prevent net negative membership growth.

The studio's immediate focus centres on retention optimisation and conversion execution. With attrition rates above target creating net membership losses despite reasonable sales activity, implementing systematic retention protocols through Recovr software and the STRONG Challenge initiative should help reduce cancellations and improve member stickiness. The January intro offer surge (109 units) positions Leppington for potential membership growth in February, provided conversion processes effectively transition triallists from starter passes to full memberships. Addressing the attendance utilisation gap through improved booking behaviour and reduced late cancels will strengthen revenue per visit metrics while building the attendance habits that support long-term retention. The FOH restructure and ongoing sales training provide operational frameworks to support these efforts, with success dependent on consistent execution of established processes and systematic member engagement.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  • January's 109 intro offer sales from STRONG Starter Pass promotion creates conversion opportunity for mid-late February membership growth if processes execute effectively
  • Booking utilisation achieved optimal range (70-85%) in Nov and Jan, but attendance utilisation remained below healthy threshold (70-75%) throughout quarter with 8-9 point booking-to-attendance gap
  • Path forward focuses on retention optimisation through Recovr and STRONG Challenge, conversion execution from starter pass pipeline, and attendance behaviour improvement to strengthen member stickiness
