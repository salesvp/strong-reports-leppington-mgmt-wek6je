EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Membership

329 total members as of December 2025 (236 active, 93 suspended), with active rate at 71.7%. January shows early recovery with 246 active members and suspended count reduced to 75, lifting active rate to 76.6%.

Revenue

Q4 2025 generated $218,915 in gross revenue, with membership revenue comprising 85.2% ($186,439). November peaked at $77,777 driven by Black Friday pack sales ($14,737 in package revenue). December settled at $65,415 reflecting typical holiday slowdown.

Utilisation

57.42% in December, down from 63.96% in October, falling below the 70-75% optimal target. January recovered to 62.85%, indicating members returning to regular attendance patterns.

Sales vs Cancellations

Q4 totalled 49 memberships sold against 58 cancellations (net -9). December achieved exceptional 4.79% attrition, with January improving further to 3.37%. Intro offers surged 239% in January (23 to 78), signalling pipeline rebuild.

Key Issues

  • Membership contraction: Total members declined 2.7% across Q4 (338 to 329), with active members down 9.9% (262 to 236) due to December suspension spike
  • Utilisation below target: Dropped to 57.42% in December, 12-17 percentage points below the 70-75% optimal range
  • Weakened Q4 conversion pipeline: Membership sales down 48% (21 to 11), intro offers down 44% (41 to 23) reflecting end-of-year seasonality and Black Friday pack focus
  • Market vulnerability: Need to get ahead of potential market disruptions or declines before they impact the business
  • Operational consistency gap: FOH framework not yet at full implementation, limiting systematic execution of fundamentals

Active Interventions Being Deployed

  • FOH operational framework rollout: Restructured role systemises and creates consistency across acquisition, retention, socials, engagement, rewards, events, challenges, community, and longevity fundamentals—completing the full operational loop
  • Starter Pass promotion: $50 for 15 days unlimited (on sale until mid-February) with 5-session attendance incentive to drive top-of-funnel acquisition and convert to ongoing memberships
  • STRONG Challenge: Launching February to drive engagement, excitement, and team building across the member base
  • Recovr retention software: Ongoing training and active use for automated member engagement, attendance tracking, and systematic retention workflows
  • Pipeline sales process training: Systems training rolled out to improve trial-to-membership conversion consistency
  • February member survey: Gathering member and prospect insights to calibrate acquisition and retention approaches

Expected Outcomes

  • Get ahead of potential market disruptions through proactive, systematic execution of FOH fundamentals
  • Achieve operational consistency with FOH framework at full capacity, covering all required tasks across acquisition, retention, and engagement
  • Rebuild active member base toward 80% active rate target through suspension reactivation and Starter Pass conversions
  • Maintain strong retention performance with attrition at or below sub-7% target (currently tracking at exceptional 3.37% in January)
  • Reverse utilisation decline toward 70-75% optimal range through STRONG Challenge engagement and Recovr-driven reengagement
  • Optimise pipeline conversion rates as seasonal patterns normalise and FOH processes become fully operational

The active rate improved to 76.6%, nearly returning to October levels. While total membership continued to decline slightly to 321, the suspension reactivation trend and active member recovery indicate the seasonal headwinds are subsiding. SUMMARY & PATH FORWARD

Q4 2025 presented expected seasonal challenges for STRONG Pilates Leppington, with the membership base contracting modestly (338 to 329 total members) and active members declining more significantly due to the December suspension spike. The quarter generated $218,915 in gross revenue, with membership revenue comprising 85.2% of the total. The Black Friday promotional campaign proved highly effective, driving November pack sales to 97 units and contributing to the revenue peak of $77,777. Retention remained a consistent strength throughout the quarter—December's 4.79% attrition rate was exceptional, and the studio maintained performance near or below the sub-7% target across all three months despite seasonal headwinds. Utilisation declined to 57.42% by December, falling below the 70-75% optimal range as reduced active membership and holiday schedules impacted attendance patterns.

January 2026 data reveals encouraging early signs across multiple metrics: suspended members dropped 19% (93 to 75), active members rebounded 4.2% (236 to 246), intro offers surged 239% (23 to 78), and attrition improved to an outstanding 3.37%. The path forward centres on getting ahead of any potential market disruptions or declines by implementing the full FOH operational framework. This restructured role systemises and creates consistency across the fundamentals: acquisition, retention, socials, engagement, rewards, events, challenges, community, and longevity. With the Starter Pass promotion driving top-of-funnel activity and the STRONG Challenge launching in February, the focus is ensuring these initiatives operate within a structured, repeatable process rather than ad-hoc execution. The member survey planned for February will provide insights to further calibrate these systems. Success in Q1 will be measured by the FOH framework operating at full capacity, maintaining sub-7% attrition, and rebuilding the active member base toward the 80% target.

Key Takeaways

  • Q4 delivered $218,915 gross revenue with strong retention (Dec attrition 4.79%) despite seasonal membership contraction
  • January shows positive momentum: suspensions down 19%, intro offers up 239%, attrition at exceptional 3.37%
  • Priority is getting ahead of potential market disruptions through full FOH framework implementation
  • FOH role systemises acquisition, retention, socials, engagement, rewards, events, challenges, community, and longevity fundamentals
